# Source-pinned CC0 Brutalist grammar · second independent Necrospire lineage

Original author: **nosycat**, OpenGameArt *Brutalist architecture models*,
CC0. Source page: https://opengameart.org/content/brutalist-architecture-models
Archive: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/brutalism2.zip
SHA-256: `8a89504f2abf355f56f4ea93c9a50fbdc60fb3664bdad019c65e6582a91ff9a3`.

The original 2022 package contains **eight authored OpenSCAD generators**,
not a general abstract grammar DSL. The exact CC0 source is fetched, hash
checked and compiled independently as a structural reference in CI.
Selected repeated floor-band and vertical-pier motifs inspired by the
original `institute1.scad` are translated into bounded, independent
CadQuery BRep additions to the **upper keep**, with their own inheritable
activation, cadence and projection loci. Source STL is never treated as
authoritative solid CAD or imported as an equivalent structural construction.

Second fresh lineage uses source RGBA, independent L8 alpha, the Ur interpretive
ziggurat proxy, plus the pinned nosycat CC0 rules as an **active**
native detail-capability. Its own new seed differs from the first lineage.
The other source image and CAD construction truth gates remain fixed.

CI runs three OODA-HA generations, 54 proposed births, at most 36 exact
auditions and 18 detailed scores, 2x source camera overscan and bounded
mesh/depth budgets. It compares the original fresh three-generation archive
against the new six-member review on **common** source alpha IoU, source global
loss and extra-frame silhouette. Do not compare the old advisory loss directly
with the new five-locus grammar policy. Different random seeds and expanded
gene space prevent a causal assertion; a true matched-seed ablation remains
a separate experiment.

The earlier six-generation pilot never becomes an ancestor and is untouched.
