# Experiment Zero: source-frozen primary-massing evolution

The original 1024×1536 RGBA Oblique Seed and its L8 alpha share one image
identity. RGB and alpha never count as separate source cameras.

A single six-family OODA-HA controller inherits eleven native primary-massing
loci, moving the terrace, keep and crown centers and proposing independently
bounded side towers. Every proposed solid must pass the original CAD BRep,
contact and void gates before source comparison or lineage inheritance.

Five additional seed-frozen orthographic projections reveal synthetic CAD
morphology and distinguish offspring that coincide in the artwork's oblique
view. Their diversity can reserve archive/gallery places, but they do not
constitute independent source testimony or an unseen-rear reconstruction.

The hosted campaign performs three generations with 72 proposed offspring,
up to 36 exact native auditions and 18 detailed scoring seats. The bounded
search uses 128×192 source thumbnails to avoid full-resolution CAD raster
cost on every proposal. After all epochs, the exact original camera and
1024×1536 alpha independently evaluate every *scored* native-valid
offspring. No lower-resolution figure may be called a full-resolution result.
The final score never retroactively changes archived parent choices.

Every rejection and error remains available in immutable epoch receipts.
The final gallery and independent full-resolution ranking are proposals for
Operator inspection, not automatic canonical architecture or pet-name grants.

If the run fails, report the exact SHA, failed workflow step and actual
partial receipts; do not invent an aesthetic winner or call setup validation
proof of architectural improvement.
