# Positive solids, negative volumes, and evidence frames

## Structural admissibility

A project builder grows authored or nameless volumes from its genome. Every child makes a
measurable positive-volume intersection with its declared parent. The full
construction must Boolean-fuse into one valid solid before a candidate can
receive any fitness score. Independently authored empty volumes then carve
rooms, apertures, stairs and passage spaces from the fused positive body.

An explicit void graph states claimed traversals. The void audit checks that
cutters contact the material, declared links overlap, every functional space
connects to an exterior opening, and carving leaves one valid material solid.
This establishes modeled connectivity, not human walkability or real loads.

The material BRep controls production STEP, OBJ, render mesh and all four
technical illustration passes. Per-part uncarved geometry may appear in a
separate diagnostic only; it must not substitute for carved production media.

## Reference registration

A reference image used for fitness must name a fixed camera, its pixel scale,
world anchor, center, and reference dimensions. Camera registration is an
independent evidence question. A frozen viewport can prevent per-candidate
rubber-camera scoring while still being unverified against the artist source.

For projects without external imagery, construct the source object once and
lock its presentation camera to that seed. All subsequent genomes use that
same viewport for comparison. Null and asymmetric landmark fixtures test the
projection algorithm separately from subject-specific artistic inference.

## Golden principles

- Construction relationships and optional stable rule slots survive regrowth; pet-names do not control CAD face identities.
- Positive and negative geometry describe one material body and one route graph.
- Viewports are fixed for evidence and adaptive only for presentation.
- Candidate validity precedes all aesthetic ranking.
- The artwork's RGBA gradients provide uncertain interpretive evidence, never
  evidence of a fused structure or a guaranteed physical joint.
- Output drawings and STEP geometry must share a single carved material witness.

## Anti-patterns and tests

- Fitted camera per candidate: hides scale changes; use a fixed evidence frame.
- Rendering uncut parts after BRep cutting: hides entrances; compare exported
  control silhouettes with the carved render transport.
- Joint-only audit in evolution: admits unfusible candidates; require union.
- Hidden stale subject models in reusable tests: use current neutral fixtures.
- Unrecorded Boolean healing: can hide geometry defects; retain tolerance and
  repair receipts separately before enabling correction.

## Research anchors

- OpenCascade Boolean operations, shape validation and `ShapeAnalysis_ShapeTolerance`.
- OpenCascade `HLRBRep_Algo`: exact BRep edge classification for future drafting.
- Mouret and Clune / pyribs: illuminate diverse architecture via descriptor
  archives rather than collapsing candidate selection to a single parameter
  distance.
- Persistent topology identification review: keep authored feature names
  stable; do not rely on post-Boolean face sequence numbers.

## Relation and name boundary

The builder may grow anonymous children and voids with descriptive `#has`
capabilities. A relational witness captures parentage, role, capability and
explicit rule slot. A separate local-first ledger retains operator pet-names and
location-set names. See `relational-identity.md` for o-takiage releases and
reconciliation receipts. Neither pet-naming nor o-takiage changes the material
or traversal validation gates.
