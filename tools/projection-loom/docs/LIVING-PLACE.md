# N8 · The Place That Grows · source and intent boundaries

This contract records a design intention, not a language model runtime.
The operator and current Fire interpret ordinary art direction, then explicitly
author an `IntentContract` and the separately digest-bound `SpatialIntent`.
No art text executes, no source camera changes, and no unaudited geometry
acquires an approved relation.

## Three distinct authorities

- `SpatialGoal` names a falsifiable geometric hypothesis. `connect` has one
  limited native witness in N8-C. `shelter` and `ascend` deliberately return
  `unverified`, never a guessed success. Future implementations must attach
  typed original/native and withheld-counterfactual evidence before admission.
- `PlaceGenome` carries two to sixteen separately inherited `GrowthProgram`
  bodies and bounded named walkways. No scene-wide Boolean union, stable face
  IDs, or mandatory artistic pet-name enters the inheritance.
- `place_affordances.assess_place` reimports independently packaged STEP,
  verifies **both horizontal BRep bearings** and a bounded unoccupied
  eastward axis-aligned agent headroom corridor. This does not measure human
  accessibility, full route planning, engineered load safety or a survey.
  `verify_place_affordances` repeats the native evidence, rejecting changed
  reports rather than trusting visual metadata.

## Design-time consent

The following statements may *only* affect downstream construction after Fire
authors a typed binding. A picture of a bridge does not establish a path.
An imagined refuge does not establish an enclosed court. The operator chooses
which unknowns to retain and whether an unverified goal remains useful as an
artistic study. In particular, reward must not reduce to part count.

## Implementation aftermath

N8-A/B/C implement typed interpretation, multi-body genomes and an actual
two-bearing crossing probe. The initially implemented crossing supports only
an explicitly declared eastward route through one deck. Broader site traversal
and alternative goals remain unimplemented until individually witnessed.
Every N8 claim requires exact-head CI; no new pet-name, canon promotion,
Unreal Editor success, physical certification or implicit PR merge follows.

Experiment anchor: `tests/test_hephaestus_place_affordances.py`.

## N8 · Aftermath II · Source to generation without a shadow controller

The current campaign runner calls the existing `OODAHAJournal`; it does not
install a model interpreter, new five-phase authority, or one-run camera score.
An `IntentContract` authorizes the run identity, a separately authored spatial
binding constrains construction, and each accepted `PlaceGenome` regrows from
its own immutable program into a source/causal/native `location_workspace`.
The inherited place contains independent solids, not one bogus global union.

`spatial_niches` and individually attributed candidate receipts preserve
several geometrically distinct interpretations. Morphology does not grant
truth: every descendant independently undergoes exact STEP bearings and
bounded corridor headroom. A candidate that lacks a complete spatial witness
keeps `spatial-unverified` status instead of receiving a visual-loss penalty
or claiming native failure. An unattempted proposal remains deferred under
finite budget rather than falsely rejected. The operator chooses what to
retain; a niche does not select an artistic winner.

Known limits: native route witness currently implements one eastward walkway
and an axis-aligned agent envelope. The inherited construction grammar still
uses `foundation/spawn/split/bridge/carve` within each body. The study does
not infer unobserved real-world scale, certified accessibility, load,
human experience, or survey truth. A review or export cannot promote canon.

## N8 · Intent aftermath IV · A relation makes a change, not a label

The new `propose_from_spatial_intent` creates a bounded additional
construction body ONLY when the operator/Fire explicitly author a `connect`
goal between two known, simple rooted support sites. Their authored dimensions
and placements determine the deck's constructive program, not image pixels,
language tokens, or arbitrary OpenCascade face identifiers. Existing declared
walkways remain untouched. The original genome and the constructed genome
each keep full separate content digests in the preregistration.

North/south and either horizontal direction use the same native bearing test;
diagonal and different-level supports still refuse. `shelter` and `ascend`
continue as explicit unimplemented architectural hypotheses. This limited
contrast establishes genuine intent sensitivity for a finite case, not
generalized text-to-architecture or human interpretation of a sanctuary.
Operator and Fire attribution describes the authored record; it does not
cryptographically authenticate its human author.

The native corridor uses a bounded axis-aligned agent envelope; no formula
silently turns that witness into building-code accessibility or physical
load-bearing certification. Equal camera/source testimonies survive unchanged.
