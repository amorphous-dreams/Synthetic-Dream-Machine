# N8 · The Place That Grows · source and intent boundaries

This contract records a design intention, not a language model runtime.
The operator and current Fire interpret ordinary art direction, then explicitly
author an `IntentContract` and the separately digest-bound `SpatialIntent`.
No art text executes, no source camera changes, and no unaudited geometry
acquires an approved relation.

## Three distinct authorities

- `SpatialGoal` names a falsifiable geometric hypothesis. `connect` has one
  limited native witness in N8-C. `shelter` and `ascend` deliberately return
  `unverified`, never a guessed success. Future implementations must attach
  typed original/native and withheld-counterfactual evidence before admission.
- `PlaceGenome` carries two to sixteen separately inherited `GrowthProgram`
  bodies and bounded named walkways. No scene-wide Boolean union, stable face
  IDs, or mandatory artistic pet-name enters the inheritance.
- `place_affordances.assess_place` reimports independently packaged STEP,
  verifies **both horizontal BRep bearings** and a bounded unoccupied
  eastward axis-aligned agent headroom corridor. This does not measure human
  accessibility, full route planning, engineered load safety or a survey.
  `verify_place_affordances` repeats the native evidence, rejecting changed
  reports rather than trusting visual metadata.

## Design-time consent

The following statements may *only* affect downstream construction after Fire
authors a typed binding. A picture of a bridge does not establish a path.
An imagined refuge does not establish an enclosed court. The operator chooses
which unknowns to retain and whether an unverified goal remains useful as an
artistic study. In particular, reward must not reduce to part count.
