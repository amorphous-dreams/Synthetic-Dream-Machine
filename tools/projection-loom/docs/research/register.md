The active subsystems now cover:

* `projection_loom.location_stack`: evidence/source/frame relationships and multiple separately audited CAD bodies;
* `projection_loom.geospatial`: actual digest-checked raster metadata, valid masks, projected CRS and bounded terrain sampling;
* `projection_loom.surface_witness`: real XYZ/OBJ witness sampling and explicitly approximate point-cloud residuals;
* `projection_loom.registration3d`: rigid control-point fits, with file-backed holdout evidence;
* `projection_loom.grammar` and `projection_loom.rewriting`: CAD growth and hypothesis graph proposals;
* `projection_loom.epochs`: staged, resumable source-identified multi-method search.

Research boundaries remain explicit: no single photo proves absolute scale;
no DEM declares its real-world height datum merely by having an EPSG horizontal
code; no manually supplied correspondences certify a survey; no graph rewrite
claims actual geometry until CAD validation; no preview pass certifies a solid.
Full SfM, bundle adjustment, LAS/LAZ, IFC, OpenUSD, exact BRep hidden-line
export and true next-best-view optimization remain separate integrations.

## Prior art by operational seam

- **Measurement**: Rasterio [quickstart](https://rasterio.readthedocs.io/en/stable/quickstart.html), [mask semantics](https://rasterio.readthedocs.io/en/stable/topics/masks.html); [pyproj transformation availability](https://pyproj4.github.io/pyproj/stable/advanced_examples.html).
- **Space**: buildingSMART [IFC openings](https://standards.buildingsmart.org/IFC/RELEASE/IFC4/FINAL/HTML/schema/ifcproductextension/lexical/ifcopeningelement.htm); [CityGML](https://www.ogc.org/standards/citygml/).
- **Composition**: [OpenUSD composition arcs](https://openusd.org/25.11/glossary.html).
- **Inference**: [GTSAM factor-graph introduction](https://gtsam.org/tutorials/intro.html); [COLMAP](https://colmap.github.io/).
- **Grammar**: [ShapeAssembly](https://doi.org/10.1145/3414685.3417812); [Szalinski](https://doi.org/10.1145/3385412.3386012); [ShapeCoder](https://doi.org/10.1145/3592416).
- **Evolution**: [pyribs scheduler](https://docs.pyribs.org/en/latest/api/ribs.schedulers.Scheduler.html) and [bandit schedulers](https://docs.pyribs.org/en/latest/api/ribs.schedulers.BanditScheduler.html).
- **Provenance**: [W3C PROV primer](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-primer/).

The archival links tell **what we tried**. Current code/tests tell **what works now**.
