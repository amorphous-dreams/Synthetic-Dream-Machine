# Projection Loom — Relational Emergence / Pet-Names / O-Takiage

## Intent

An authored construction grammar may let additional materials, voids, and
attached structures appear **without names**. A post-growth witness records
relational topology and `#has` capabilities. The operator may pet-name one unique
observation or name a location as a dynamic set of nameless entities. The label
lives in a separate local ledger; its optional evolutionary retention remains
weak, capped and reversible.

## Invariants

- CAD-positive contact, fused-solid validity, void connectivity and reference
  camera constraints take precedence over all naming/novelty preferences.
- Anonymous handles are temporary graph addresses, not essences. Authored stable
  `slot` values mark rule positions, not absolute identity or a pet-name.
- Ambiguous reidentification never silently picks a lookalike. Changed relation
  with retained rule slot yields an explicit `drifted` result.
- A pet-named location groups descendants dynamically. Members may remain wholly
  nameless and hold `#has` capabilities.
- O-takiage releases a soft claim or an entire graph fork, adjusting any
  ancestor location claim to exclude that fork without deleting the location.
  All claim revisions and releases remain auditable.
- A limited phenotype-slack reserve favors valid unusual structures without
  overruling the objective or structural gates.
- The ledger supplies local optimistic conflict detection and monotonic event
  sequence; no global clock or automatic distributed merge is presumed.

## Tests and experiment

- 75 regression tests passed across the active local suite.
- End-to-end CLI: discover → pet-name → grow/export → o-takiage succeeds.
- Naming a bridge applies 0.025 extra fitness loss when a future model omits it,
  and zero extra loss when that name is released, with unchanged CAD validity.
- Seeded 9-candidate evolutionary pilot: 9 valid CAD proposals, three distinct
  relation/phenotype signatures, three retained lineages spanning two phenotype
  signatures. This is a bounded test of the survivor reserve, not evidence of
  open-ended evolution in general.

## Current limitations

- The grammar must actually contain alternate growth operations; a novelty
  reserve cannot invent entities from a closed builder.
- Slotless identical siblings remain unresolved; the operator must provide a
  distinguishing relation or rule slot before persistence.
- The location grouping follows authored parent/host relations. A geometrically
  intersecting but undeclared region may not appear in the group.
- `#has` reports authored attributes, not independently measured accessibility,
  engineering capacity, or geometry-derived semantics.
- Future multiuser sync would require stronger causal merge semantics than
  the single-file optimistic conflict detector.
- Boot-seed names and the traditional Japanese rite inform the metaphor; the
  software procedure neither enacts a rite nor alters physical simulation laws.
