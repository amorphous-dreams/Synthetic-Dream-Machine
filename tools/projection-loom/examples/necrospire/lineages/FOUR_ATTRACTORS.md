# Four-Attractor Necrospire · Fresh Experimental Lineage

Four distinct sources enter one new source-bound breeding lineage:

1. **Original RGBA oblique**: 1024×1536 painting, composition and color/edge witness.
2. **Archived L8 oblique alpha**: independent matching silhouette source and fixed-camera fitness.
3. **Ur ziggurat proxy**: interpretive staged massing / height ratios from self-authored CAD fixture.
4. **Brutalist procedural rules**: self-authored podium, cantilever and modular massing analogies.

The guide transforms only normalized, bounded seed loci. Physical dimensions
from archaeological approximations never transfer into Necrospire's unitless CAD.
If that guided seed fails native CAD, bounded relaxation toward the original
source seed selects the first independently admitted candidate. Four-source
provenance travels with SHA-256 in inputs/manifest.json.

The existing OODA-HA helm then grows **three new generations** (54 births,
up to 36 exact CAD auditions and 18 detailed scores), with 2× viewport overscan
and finite depth, vertex and face budgets. Each candidate retains source-view
loss, separate outer-frame observations, topology witnesses and 3D review views.
Only newly scored descendants enter the new lineage archive. The six-generation
pilot remains read-only and excluded from ancestry or score comparison.

Post-run four_attractor_review.json measures original alpha IoU, unitless
Ur/brutalist locus proximity, weak RGBA boundary agreement and expansion
novelty. The recommendation remains **advisory**; Operator approval and naming
require a separate action. No result claims physically accurate archaeology,
camera registration, material identity, or real-world structural proof.

Run with the dedicated
[GitHub Actions workflow](../../../../../.github/workflows/necrospire-four-attractor-lineage.yml).
Artifact ZIP carries full native journals, STEP/OBJ and metadata while a small
contact sheet / review receipt land on the lineage branch.
