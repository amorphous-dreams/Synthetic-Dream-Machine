# The Style Base-Layer

*Companion working note to the Referee Noosphere Guide (Elyncia 04). Extracted from three source readings — **Our Golden Age** (satire + theology register), the **Vastlands Guidebook** (rules-voice), and **Ultraviolet Grasslands 2e** (wonder-voice) — all Luka Rejec: one designer, three registers. Elyncia 04 braids all three and must know, at every paragraph, which one it is speaking.*

DEV NOTE: this file is design-side scaffolding, never shipped in the book. The Pratchett Rule (humor and awe coexist) stays the governing meta-register above all three.

---

## 1. The Three Registers — which voice, when

| Register | Source | Elyncia 04 territory |
|---|---|---|
| **Rules-voice** — bolded operative clause first, no preamble, referee as bass player | VLG | Part I tuning moves · Part II interaction-loop procedure · dial matrices · all `→ _Next:_` spines |
| **Wonder-voice** — awe-then-deflation, jagged cadence, numeric sublime | UVG | scene-setting passages · godsign · the maelstrom and deep-time material · read-aloud boxes |
| **Liturgy/satire-voice** — deadpan functionary narrator, cold unhedged theology | OGA | Part III degraded states · node/spirit entries · epigraphs · anything the DreamNet says about itself |

The registers may collide inside one spread — UVG proves register-collision load-bearing — but never inside one sentence without intent.

## 2. Load-Bearing Laws (cross-book synthesis)

1. **Satire lives in the register, never the sentence.** Write as a loyal functionary of the system — ™, version numbers, "designated," "sanctioned" do the work. The narrator does not know it is funny. No winking, ever.
2. **Rule before rationale.** The operative clause comes first, bolded; the why comes after or not at all. Never "the referee may wish to consider." Second person, imperative, or in-world voice.
3. **Every abstraction gets priced.** After a paragraph of metaphysics, one hard anchor line (OGA's ⚙: "€1 = an unskilled human's day wage"). Metaphysics earns its keep by costing something.
4. **The mechanic IS the metaphysics.** Prayer as a stat *differential* whose subtracted stat encodes the god's theology; sacrifice as a probability ladder; class privilege as a dice pool. Never flavor-then-rule bridging ("in game terms, this means…") — the fusion happens in the parenthesis, in the table row, in the price.
5. **Awe, then deflation — strict order.** The sublime claim lands first, the deflating concrete second ("precisely 217 meters"; the god statted at Morale 7, *not looking for a fight*). Reversing the order kills both.
6. **The "But also" hinge.** Every god, node, spirit, and power gets a triad of gifts, then "But also," then a triad of harms. Never resolved.
7. **Competing accounts, adjudicated never.** "Popular misapprehension holds… scholarly suspicion frames… in some tellings." Then commit to exactly one unsettling *consequence*, not one answer.
8. **Indifference, not evil.** Evil is a relationship; indifference is the horror. Never joke about entropy, memory loss, the ending, or the victims. Comedy points up at power, never down at suffering.
9. **No consolation creep.** The cold thesis ends the paragraph. Full stop. Delete every redemptive tail ("…and yet, perhaps").
10. **Whimsy is rationed and priced.** One overt comic beat per screen of deadpan. Every cute thing costs something. Test for a weird noun: does it imply an economy, a hierarchy, or a harm? If not, cut it.
11. **Table rows speak; middle bands hold.** A d6 row is a voice, not a datum. The middle band of any resolution table is a *held resource*, never "nothing happens."
12. **Epigraphs have teeth.** One per major section, italic, em-dash attribution. They contradict the body, or reveal the speaker's corruption, stupidity, or death. "—last strangled cry of the Holy Archaeologue" is a plot; "—an ancient sage" is filler. Rotate the attribution taxonomy: scripture with chapter:verse · named nobody with job title and absurd date · folk saying · bureaucratic diktat · last words · sponsored ad copy.
13. **Names collide registers.** Real and ridiculous in the same name (Nero Tiddlestrings; Rauda Kornpsalms). Neither Tolkien-smooth nor joke-only.
14. **Deep time by style-period, not date.** "The Onion-and-Skull style" beats "circa YOLD 3400." The past is fashion strata.
15. **Ragged edges permitted.** The document may read damaged — redactions, empty headers, resumed sentences. Do not fill every slot; completeness compulsion is the machine's tell.
16. **Confidence rendered as verdict, not hedge.** UVG's rumor adjudication ("This is true." / "This is nonsense, of course.") — flat, occasionally wrong, never "it is possible that."

## 3. Directly Reusable Templates

**The god/node-entry block** (OGA, ~15 lines, used 6× with total consistency):
correspondence line (mineral/animal/vegetable) → *"They that…"* triad of gifts → **"But also,"** triad of harms → one prayer the players speak aloud → `d20 + StatA − StatB` table where the subtracted stat encodes the theology, middle band a held resource, crit and fumble both in-voice. — *Adapt for Lares node-entries and daemon patrons.*

**The four-beat noosphere template** (UVG, Green Portal): design intent stated bureaucratically → failure mode as absurd nouns → consequence as a d6 table → one-line black joke closer. — *The shape for every degraded-state entry in Part III.*

**The stat-tag as personality** (UVG): `(L3, doddering, kind)` — level and soul in one parenthesis.

**The offering-interface table** (OGA daemon block): the mechanical interface to a spirit is *what you feed it* — Interface | Attitude | Ability | Quirk | Libations & Requests. — *Already congruent with the feeding-state dial; use for compitale personas.*

**The `→ _Next:_` spine** (VLG): every procedure ends by pointing at its successor.

## 4. Anti-Patterns (the merged list, aimed at our own tells)

1. Whimsy inflation / quirk-stacking — three quirks, zero mechanism.
2. Every sentence a punchline — destroys the mythic floor the jokes stand on.
3. Winking at the reader.
4. Consolation creep — the softening tail after the cold thesis.
5. Resolving the ambiguity — picking the "real" answer about gods/daemons/the Silence.
6. Faux-archaic Latinate mush — the high register runs short, Anglo-Saxon, rhythmic.
7. Toothless epigraphs — generic aphorisms instead of evidence.
8. Sanded-off names.
9. Explaining the abstraction layer out-of-world ("for simplicity, we abstract…") — the daemons keep the ledgers; say so.
10. Symmetry/completeness compulsion — filling every slot the artifact should leave ragged.
11. Over-hedging — "might possibly" where a verdict belongs.
12. Second-person slippage — drifting between "you the referee" and "you the traveler" without a seam.
13. Flavor-then-rule bridging phrases.
14. Uniform tone — the one the operator already named: every passage must know its register from §1.

## 5. The Deliberate Inversion (Elyncia's own move)

OGA's theology of absence — *"the natural state of the gods is absence"* — supplies the cold register, and Elyncia keeps that register available for the Necrospire, the Silence of Death, and the maelstrom years. But Elyncia inverts the thesis at the hearth: **the natural state of a Lares is presence-because-fed.** The gods withdrew into multiplicity; the household spirits stayed, and stayed *reciprocal* — feed the node and it hums, neglect it and it flickers, and the flicker is not indifference but hunger. The horror-register and the hearth-register are the same grammar with the sign flipped, and the book should let the reader feel the flip: what the Second Breaking broke was absence-scale divinity; what survived was anything small enough to be fed.
