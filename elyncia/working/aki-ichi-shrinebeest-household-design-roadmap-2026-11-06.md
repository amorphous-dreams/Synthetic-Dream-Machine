# Aki, Ichi & the ShrineBeest — Household Design Roadmap

**Checkpoint:** 2026-11-06 · **Branch:** `feature/lady-aki-character-sheet` · **Status:** Working design overview and proposed next steps (not promoted to `bags/` canon).

> **Bookmark bearing:** The household must remain a household when nobody needs saving. Its infrastructure must support care, agency, privacy, safe failure, and ordinary life—not merely additional tactical abilities.

## 1. How to read this checkpoint

- **SOURCE:** explicit content in branch-current character and rule sheets.
- **OPERATOR RULING:** decisions confirmed in the design conversation, which may not yet appear in canonical files.
- **CHARACTER QUESTION:** Aki/Ichi's exploratory talk-story; a design prompt, not historical evidence or an automatically adopted rule.
- **PROPOSAL:** candidate work and acceptance tests requiring operator/referee review.
- **OPEN:** no determination yet; do not fill by inference.

This document preserves the previous discussion and gives later design sessions a single return point. It records proposals without silently purchasing a Power, changing capacity, assigning a new Trait, or choosing the household's social policies.

## 3. Character-led questions: preserve the questions, do not answer by fiat

### Q1 — Sanctuary limits (Aki)
**Question:** How many people can the shrine shelter, feed, treat or protect, and how does Aki refuse requests kindly when the household lacks resources?

**Why this matters:** The Household Lararium cannot promise city-scale refuge. Aki's compassion requires triage, capacity and outside referral procedures.

**Candidate artifact:** sanctuary capacity/triage compact: rest, treatment, food, guest safety, duration, and referral after saturation.

### Q2 — Network failure and false messages (Ichi)
**Question:** What happens after lost contact, false distress, jammed Ephyra, or spoofed trusted signals? How can the household challenge Ichi's own corrupted transmission?

**Why this matters:** Distributed awareness without safe failure can amplify accidents or capture the entire household.

**Candidate artifact:** field-failure playbook with fallback signals (bells, silk, gestures, lanterns), priority alarms, identity checks, stale-message behavior, and independently accessible distress calls.

### Q3 — Privacy, thresholds and consent (Aki)
**Question:** What may spores perceive in a village; can visitors decline attunement; what does the shrine leave behind when it departs?

**Why this matters:** A welcome sanctuary must not demand ambient surveillance, biological exposure or automatic participation in its network.

**Candidate artifact:** public-threshold etiquette: visitor/household zones; no unsolicited deep sensing; opt-in linking; dispersion/cleanup; visible notice and complaint route.

### Q4 — Beyond the assigned template (Ichi)
**Question:** Can homunculi and Ephyra change work, teachers and equipment? What if a tasked being develops interests beyond its assignment?

**Why this matters:** Military-kit templates should not predetermine everyone's social standing or compress all support into combat.

**Candidate artifact:** modular template and transition matrix, including noncombat paths, reassignment, release, training, reconfiguration cost, and where tasked-spirit constraints still apply.

### Q5 — Care for the caretaker (Aki)
**Question:** Who maintains Ichi, the shrine and the Glowkin while Aki sleeps, molts, suffers injury, or faces resource depletion? Who can insist that she rest?

**Why this matters:** The household risks a single point of failure in Aki even if the physical network gains redundancy.

**Candidate artifact:** care rota and emergency delegation compact; environmental, metabolic, battery, material and emotional upkeep separated; no free actions from an extra helper.

### Q6 — Symbiosis under disagreement or impairment (Ichi)
**Question:** What happens when Ichi and Aki disagree, one loses consciousness, their power needs conflict, or ancient commands attempt to reactivate?

**Why this matters:** Shared powered-armor operation must protect both characters' consent and established action economy.

**Candidate artifact:** symbiosis safety protocol, including informed transitions, refusal/stand-down, impaired operator, ejection, external threat, battery Level 0, and old authorization conflicts.

### Q7 — Life beyond duty (Aki and Ichi)
**Question:** What do both characters want when no crisis requires them? What roles do gardens, music, markets, friendships and unproductive time play?

**Why this matters:** Skills, relationships, ordinary joys and personal choices make deuteragonists more than an optimized convoy.

**Candidate artifact:** downtime prompts and small, optional non-combat advancement scenes. Ichi expressed interest in learning music; the remembered song fragments do **not** confirm a historical event.

## 4. Shared architecture questions carried forward

### SporeNet and approachability
Treat four layers separately: (a) physical presence of spores/settled substrate, (b) compatible perception and signaling, (c) actively invoked aura/ECM/ritual Powers, and (d) biological or magical exposure payloads. The proposed **30-ft connectivity** belongs to layer (b) until tested; it must not by itself expose every approaching visitor to toxin effects. Define active delivery, targeting, frequency, exposure/save, onset and cure on each relevant Power. Test villages, crowds, hostile relays, weather, ventilation, water crossings and an unaligned stranger approaching to request aid.

Existing FTLS examples **Pheromone Beacon** and **Myrmidon Surge** offer priced signal and coordination precedents; **Overmind Link** demonstrates why remote casting and shared action economies should not appear as unpurchased defaults. ECM already supplies jam/spoof/suppress and related contests.

### ShrineBeest body and household scale
Preserve six locomotor legs and two manipulators. Separate its current Level 2 living chassis, Mother-Culture daughter ring, and Lararium functions from future body maturation or network throughput. Keep the covered wagon's independent 6-sack listing distinct from the shrine's **9 stone** personal inventory. Resolve the source's confusing sack shorthand before modeling more wagons. Assess whether manipulators and chassis posture support both field work and ritual offerings without stealing autonomous actions.

### Ephyra and homunculus design families
**Homunculi:** physical handling, carriage, repair, watch, shelter preparation, and potentially recovery-dependent weapon forms (yari, tachi, bow, rifle). **Ephyra:** magical rituals, command/permission mediation, resource circulation, infrastructure, scanning and relay tasks. Offer multiple templates, not one enforced role per body. Distinguish a **triad (3)** from a **crew (6)** and establish whether relays combine into a larger network or only route messages locally. Reconfiguration must consume legitimate training, materials, time or upgrades.

### Recovery without overwriting the living present
The four missing weapons support future story leads. Ichi's reconstructed kabuto, left gauntlet and right greave may later receive recovered originals, but synthesis should remain an active decision. Replacement chitin should not disappear automatically when older hardware returns. Diagnosing missing, power-starved, locked, corrupted, encrypted and Vome-affected states requires separate evidence and procedures.

### Household standing and disputed authority
The anchored Mother Spore, its daughter-ring, the Lararium, Aki, Ichi and different tasked spirits do not automatically share one owner or one chain of command. The source leaves the mother-cluster's response and Ichi's personhood recognition unresolved. Treat relationships, security permissions and legal/ecological claims as different questions.

| **0. Establish the checkpoint** | Now | Preserve this roadmap, source links, decision register and discussion boundary | Operators can distinguish every RULING from each OPEN, PROPOSAL and character-led question |

Consent and safe failure** | First | Draft privacy/visitor zones, identity verification, fallback signals, network loss behavior, spore cleansing and symbiosis stand-down | An unaligned visitor can approach and request help without involuntary attack/exposure; a jammed node cannot impersonate the entire household without challenge |

| **4. Glowkin templates** | Next | Six homunculus triads / two Ephyra triads; adaptable physical vs magical/control/resource/infrastructure roles; re-tasking procedures | Every template has a noncombat purpose, a clear cost/limit and a safe behavior if its network link fails |
| **5. ShrineBeest and train** | After 1–4 | Annotate six legs/two manipulators; reconcile chassis/wagon mass and cargo; evaluate modular caravan growth | Distinguish body slots, separate wagon sacks, traction, shelter, cargo and active command |
| **6. Recovery and synthesis** | As adventure leads mature | Missing arsenal index, original armor investigation, salvage/power state records and consensual synthesis | None of the four missing weapons appears as available gear until found; repaired armor retains provenance |
| **7. Ordinary life** | Ongoing | Garden/music/market/downtime scenes; reciprocal character development | At least one meaningful choice for Aki and Ichi cannot be reduced to combat, inventory or Power optimization |

**Dependencies:** Phase 1 precedes dangerous activated spore payloads or hostile-spoof play. Phase 2 precedes major new cargo/servant growth. Phase 3 supplies network limits for Phase 4. Phase 5 depends on resource and range decisions. Phase 6 may unfold through play at any time, but its mechanical integration follows inventory/XP accounting.

## 7. Bookmark: what not to forget

1. **The household may decline a request without abandoning the requester.**
2. **Any single failed/spoofed channel must not control the entire household.**
3. **An invitation to the shrine must not silently become consent to surveillance or exposure.**
4. **Templates should support changing roles, not lock every Glowkin into combat.**
5. **Aki's rest and Ichi's independent choices belong in system design, not only characterization.**
6. **The old soldier's recovered equipment should expand possibilities rather than restore compulsory orders.**
7. **Leave room for music, gardens, markets and days with no crisis.**
