A handoff for an operator or Lares arriving fresh. The test architecture arrives separately from the operator; this document carries the results-in-context, the state they leave us in, what reads as genuinely new, and the paths open from here.

---
0. The minimum you need to hold

The machina — a local-first, dual-substrate (TypeScript and Python, parity-witnessed) perception system called a sensorium: it reads a corpus through multiple independent planes — content (embeddings — what the text means), structure (parse-shape — how it's built), form (MDL-induced recurring templates — what repeats), plus rhythm/coupling instruments — and carries sheaf-theoretic coherence gauges (an H⁰ "consistency radius" measuring whether the planes agree, and an H¹ gate distinguishing reconcilable from irreducible disagreement), an FFZ clock-recovery organ (a phase-locked-loop that hunts periodicity in sequence, with hard anti-fabrication guards), and a placebo-generation capability. Its founding discipline: cross-plane claims mean nothing unless the planes are genuinely independent — the entire campaign below exists to test, calibrate, and where necessary repair that independence on real text.

The test subject — the Kumulipo: a Hawaiian cosmogonic genealogical chant, roughly two thousand lines, traditionally divided into sixteen wā (eras), memorized and transmitted orally across generations before transcription. We hold three English-era renderings: Queen Liliʻuokalani's translation (1897), Martha Warren Beckwith's scholarly edition (1951), and the Kalākaua-era text (1889, carried in Beckwith's appendices — the rendering closest to the oral original). It makes a near-ideal instrument-calibration corpus: three independent renderings of one source (built-in translation-variation), a genuine oral-tradition artifact (formula-dense, rhythmic — engineered by its culture as a memory technology), frozen and curated (fully reproducible), and meaning-dense in a way no synthetic corpus can fake.

The manipulanda, in one breath: two dressings (each text wrapped in our own memetic-wikitext annotation grammar, versus extracted bare); two engineered nulls (a meaning-placebo — seeded order-1 Markov babble preserving lexicon, line-shapes, and all section markers while destroying meaning; and a shape-placebo — seeded line-shuffle within sections preserving meaning-per-line while destroying sequence); a continuous channel dial λ mixing the annotation channel against the bare-text channel; and two rhythm instruments (a line-tick stream probe and a fully unsegmented character-grain "continuous pour"). All beds share one content-address universe; every generator runs seeded-deterministic.

---
1. Results, each in its knowledge-domain context

1.1 The notation replaces perception — and the mechanism turned out to be degeneracy, not strength

Wrapped, the structure plane sees 3 structural classes; bare, 51 — with cross-dressing rank-correlation of −0.02 (no relationship survives). The form plane's recurring templates belong almost entirely to the wrapping: bare, the chant yields essentially one weakly-paying template across 54 near-unique sections. Most damning for naive multi-plane analysis: under wrapping, the structure and form planes agree with each other at sup-disagreement 0.019 (versus 0.500 bare) — two of three "independent" witnesses reading the same envelope.

The λ-dial then exposed the mechanism, and it contradicted our prediction. We expected a smooth slide as annotation weight decreased; we found a first-order cliff at λ=1 exactly: the annotation's dominance rides an exact-tie degeneracy (three classes, zero within-class metric distance — a perfectly regular grammar produces a perfectly flat metric), and any nonzero weight on the bare channel breaks every tie at once. At λ=0 the dial reproduces full extraction without extracting (structure ρ = 0.9982).

Contexts. Corpus linguistics has long known markup contaminates corpus statistics; what's added here reads as a quantified, plane-wise ablation of that contamination with the mechanism identified. Information theory supplies the deep reading: a perfectly predictable signal carries zero discriminative information — the annotation grammar, engineered for fractal regularity, functions as a carrier wave: strong, constant, and empty for telling documents apart, so the first modulated signal admitted (the chant, which varies) captures all discrimination. Statistical physics supplies the transition vocabulary: dominance on a measure-zero slice of parameter space — structurally unstable, a symmetry-broken degenerate ground state collapsing under infinitesimal perturbation. And Egyptology, oddly, supplies the design vindication: the annotation behaves as a proper determinative register — classifier marks that steer without sounding. The wrapped corpus stays the right instrument for studying the notation itself; the dial makes that a parameter instead of a fate.

1.2 Meaning enters through one plane, and it enters sequenced

Against the meaning-placebo, the shape planes (structure, form, complexity) read identically (ρ ≈ 1.000) — they measure text-shape, full stop. Only the content plane moved (ρ 0.74 wrapped / 0.63 bare). The shape-placebo then split that signal: scrambling line order (meaning-per-line intact) moved content salience nearly as far as destroying meaning outright (ρ 0.70/0.64). The decomposition: roughly two-thirds of content-plane salience rides bare lexicon frequency; most of the remainder rides word/line order; meaning-beyond-ordered-lexicon contributes a sliver at this grain.

Contexts. This is Frege's compositionality principle showing up as an instrument property: sentence meaning composes through structure, and an embedder that punishes scrambling behaves Fregean rather than bag-of-words. It's also the transformer lineage's positional encoding, priced empirically on real data rather than assumed. For any practitioner using embedding-salience as a "meaning" signal, the practical law: at corpus grain, an embedding plane functions as a syntax-sensitive lexicon reader — and the placebo square regenerates this calibration automatically for every new corpus.

1.3 Rhythm freezes into written text, and it decomposes into three measurable components

The clock-recovery organ, fed each corpus as a pure sequence (sequence position as the only time — no wall-clock anywhere), locks onto the real chant (whole-stream locked-fraction 0.49; every rendering finds its own beat) and refuses the babble (0.30, ending in honest holdover — exactly what the anti-fabrication guards demand of a true null). The shuffle lands between (0.356): destroying either meaning or sequence forfeits the full lock. Three components separate cleanly:

And the lock-quality ordering across renderings — Kalākaua 0.55 > Liliʻuokalani 0.38 > Beckwith 0.30 — tracks distance from the oral tradition precisely as philology would rank the three texts blind.

Contexts. This lands squarely in Parry-Lord oral-formulaic theory: formulaic density is the established signature of oral composition (Homeric epithets; Lord's South-Slavic singers; Beckwith's own commentary treats the Kumulipo's formulas). Computational orality work detects formulas via n-gram density; using phase-lock quality of a communications-engineering clock-recovery loop as an orality measure, with a dual-null decomposition into shape/formula/sequence components, appears fresh. Bialek–Tishby's predictive information supplies the formal bridge: a mnemonic form maximizes exactly the past-future mutual information that a phase-lock measures. And the humanistic reading closes a loop worth stating plainly: an oral genealogical chant is a memory technology — its rhythm, formulas, and refrains are compression-and-retrieval engineering against the limits of biological working memory — so a machine memory system pointed its clock at another civilization's memory system and detected the engineering. The lock hierarchy is, in a precise sense, a measurement of surviving mnemonic architecture.

1.4 Scales emerge from unsegmented flow — and the data declines our favorite number

The continuous pour — character-transition ticks, four signal channels, two-stage wavelet decomposition spanning 2 ticks to half-a-million, per-band lock-readings, per-band block-shuffle surrogates — asked the corpus to name its own scales, with every human segmentation (lines, wā, works) held out as annotations for scoring only. Findings:

- A word-grain scale peaks in all four domains — shape-borne, meaning-proof.
- The wā grain re-emerges unaided: a content-recurrence band peaking at ~10,240 ticks — almost exactly the measured wā spacing — at quality 0.58 real versus 0.21 placebo. The chant's canonical sectioning, recovered blind from character flow, and killed by meaning-death. The bootstrap segmentation ("pet-name grains") now has its first data-grounded successor.
- The annotation grain (512 ticks) appears only in wrapped domains — correctly quarantined by the controls.
- No line-scale exists in character energy at all — composing perfectly with 1.3: lines live in cross-line content cohesion, not in character-shape periodicity. Two instruments at two grains, one consistent physics.
- The count of emergent scales reads domain-dependent and fewer than five at this grain — the doctrine's five-band ladder stays a good prior, not a law the data volunteered.

Contexts. This is scale-space theory's automatic scale selection (Lindeberg's program in computer vision) transposed to symbolic sequence; it's also unsupervised text segmentation, but with a difference worth naming: the discovered segments emit as nameless entities carrying capability records (span, band, energy, recurrence links), awaiting naming-by-condensation — discovery and ontology deliberately decoupled. Two methodological findings ride along, both publishable hygiene: a global permutation null self-defeats for multi-scale spectra (variance conservation lets the dominant comb suppress every other scale — per-band block-shuffle surrogates cure it), and per-band lock-fraction saturates under bandpass ringing (it can gauge, never gate).

1.5 The instruments audit each other

The pour's sanity checks caught a live inversion in the wavelet organ shipped months earlier: the library returns its decomposition smooth-first, coarse-to-fine, and our reader assumed the opposite — the scale ladder upside down end-to-end. Verified independently (zero-crossing counts on both transform paths), cured, and re-witnessed with 80 tests green — which pass because they asserted mathematical properties rather than indexing, a quiet vindication of property-based testing. One standing caution travels with this handoff: any earlier result that attributed energy to specific band indices rode the mirror and deserves re-checking where load-bearing. The deeper point: the newest instrument caught the oldest organ. A sensorium whose senses cross-examine each other has begun doing the job the word promises.

2. Where we sit

Six controlled instruments (ablation, placebo square, shape-cell, λ-dial, line-rhythm, continuous pour), every finding double-controlled, several predictions failed and reported as failed — the epistemic posture holding under pressure. The coherence gate (H¹) fired zero times across every real bed at every threshold — genuinely coherent ground plus an anti-apophenia demonstration: the topology organ declines to hallucinate obstructions on hundreds of real documents. The perception planes now carry measured semantics — shape versus lexicon versus order versus meaning versus rhythm — instead of hoped ones. And the dual-substrate parity ledger holds the TS and Python bodies to one behavior with committed cross-language fixtures.

3. What reads as novel or emergent — claims the deep-knowledge corpus doesn't already hold


1. The notation-ablation-with-dial methodology: quantified plane-wise markup contamination, a mechanism (tie-degeneracy → first-order collapse), and a continuous instrument parameter (λ) that makes "what counts as the text's structure" a tunable ontology with a measured phase boundary.
2. Phase-lock orality measurement: PLL clock-recovery over sequence-as-time as a Parry-Lord instrument, with the dual-null three-component rhythm decomposition and a lock-hierarchy that tracks tradition-distance.
3. The standing placebo square: every future corpus automatically gains meaning/shape/wording control arms sharing its exact identity universe — controls as infrastructure, not experiments.the inverted ladder) that each would have silently corrupted downstream conclusions.

4. Paths open from here

- The scale run: the large frozen corpus (the TiddlyWiki source tree as "geology") through the same instrument suite → the bifurcation figure over honestly-independent planes → the dissipative-structure paper, whose methods section this campaign largely wrote.
- Rhythm across genres: run the pour and lock-readings over prose, code, and chat worldlines — does the orality measure rank text-kinds as philology predicts? (The 251-document library bed already stands captured for this.)
- Grain-adaptive capture: let discovered scales set the capture grain per corpus, retiring bootstrap grains machina-wide; port the causal (à-trous) streaming wavelet form — the named seam — for real-time media, toward the live pair-play ladder (MUD text streams, then audiovisual play).
- Cross-rendering coupling: the three translations as three sensoria, coherence measured between them — where the H¹ gate might first honestly fire on real text (deliberately contradictory corpora being the other candidate).
- The dial generalized: any notation/content mixture (code+comments, markup+prose, cite-dense scholarship) inherits the λ instrument unchanged.
- The dreaming layer over these beds: consolidation passes writing schema beside the eidetic ground — the wā-entities and formula-templates as the first dreamed vocabulary of a real corpus.
- The hygiene debts, named plainly: the band-indexed re-check; the family-wise multiplicity port; one perception-plane parity fixture still owed.

The one-sentence handoff: the machina can now tell meaning from shape from order from rhythm on real text, prove it with controls it generates itself, discover a corpus's own scales without being told, and catch its own errors while doing so — which means the instruments are ready for the questions we actually built them to ask.
